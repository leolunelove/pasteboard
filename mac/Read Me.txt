PASTEBOARD 0.17.0
A visual worktable for Mac.

WORK IN PROGRESS
This is an early preview. Expect rough edges, and keep a separate backup of
important boards. The app is locally signed, not Apple-notarized.

OPEN
Download Pasteboard-Mac.zip from GitHub Releases, unzip it, and move
Pasteboard.app to a writable Applications folder. Requires an Apple Silicon
Mac running macOS 13 or later. No account is needed in the app.

UPDATE
Choose Pasteboard > Check for Updates, or App updates in the sidebar.
Update and Relaunch downloads the official GitHub release, verifies its ZIP
checksum and app identity, saves your boards, installs, and relaunches.
A failed verification leaves the installed app unchanged. The installer
restores the previous app if it cannot launch the replacement.
Checking and downloading updates requires an internet connection.

YOUR BOARDS
Boards and images save locally on your Mac. Automatic local backups and
portable board export are available in the app. Existing Gather boards remain
compatible; the storage folder and portable document extension retain their
original names so existing data does not need moving.

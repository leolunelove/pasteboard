Ready to post now sits above Workspaces and collects marked images and frames from every workspace.
The global collection shows each post’s workspace/page and opens its saved social preview and caption.
Paste, drop or add local videos with Media or Command O. Press Play or double-click a video card to open its player.
Video playback includes pause, seeking, volume and fullscreen; closing the player stops playback.
Paste YouTube watch, Shorts, live or youtu.be links to add playable video cards, including start times.
YouTube loads only when you press Play. Open on YouTube is available for videos that restrict embedding.
Videos are stored locally and included in backups and portable board copies. Page moves and duplicate cards keep their clips.
Videos and selections containing videos cannot be marked Ready to Post.
Pasteboard remains an early preview. Keep a backup of important boards.
